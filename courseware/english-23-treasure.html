📖 Treasure Hunt! 🏴‍☠️
🗺️ Steve finds an old map in a bottle. "Look! A treasure map!" he says.
🌲 Steve and Alex follow the treasure map through the dark forest. They look for clues along the way.
⛏️ "Let's dig here!" says Alex. They dig and dig in the soft ground.
📦 They open a big chest. Inside are shiny gold coins and sparkling gems!
💰 "We found the treasure! Hooray!" they cheer. So much gold!
