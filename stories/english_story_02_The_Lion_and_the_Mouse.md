# The Lion and the Mouse

Deep in the heart of the Great Green Forest lived a big, golden lion named Leo. His roar was so loud, it made the leaves on the tallest trees shake. Leo was very proud of his strong, sharp teeth and his mighty claws. He was the king of the forest, and everyone knew it.

One warm afternoon, Leo was taking a nap under a giant acacia tree. He was sleeping soundly, dreaming of chasing zebras, when a little mouse named Milly scurried across his paw.

"Mmmph! What's this?" the lion grumbled, waking up with a start.

He looked down and saw a tiny, grey mouse trembling between his giant paws. Milly had been running so fast, she didn't see the sleeping lion at all.

"Oh, Mr. Lion! I am so sorry!" squeaked Milly. "Please don't eat me!"

Leo could have laughed. A mouse as a snack? He was a hunter of wildebeest! But he was still cross about his nap.

"You should be sorry," he rumbled, his voice like thunder. "Disturbing the king of the forest! I ought to teach you a lesson."

He opened his big, big mouth, showing all his gleaming teeth.

"Please!" cried Milly. Her little heart was beating very fast. "Don't eat me, please! If you let me go, I promise to help you someday. I know I am small, but even small creatures can be a big help!"

Leo stopped. The idea was so funny that he purred with amusement. A tiny mouse like her? Help *him*, the strongest lion in the whole forest? It was the silliest thing he had ever heard.

"You? Help me?" he chuckled, shaking his mane. "That is very funny. Alright then, little one. I am in a good mood. You may go. But don't let me catch you near my nap again!"

And he lifted his paw. Milly was so happy, she gave a little thank-you squeak and ran off into the long grass.

A few days later, something bad happened. Some hunters came to the forest. They set up a strong net and placed tasty fruit as a trap. Leo, following the scent of mangoes, walked right into it!

The net fell over him—*whump!* He roared and struggled, but the more he moved, the tighter the ropes pulled. He was trapped!

"Help! Someone, help!" boomed the lion's voice. He roared until he was hoarse, but no one came near. They were all too scared.

Hiding in a bush, Milly heard the mighty roars of pain and frustration. She knew that voice. It was the lion from under the tree!

She scampered over to the net and peeked through. "Mr. Leo! Is that you?"

"Oh, it's you, little mouse," sighed the lion sadly. "Well, you see my trouble. I am caught in a hunter's net. This is the end."

"Not the end!" said Milly, her eyes bright with determination. "You let me go when I thought all was lost. Now, it is my turn to help you."

"But how?" asked Leo. "The ropes are too strong for you."

Milly didn't say a word. She put her sharp little teeth to work on one of the thick ropes. *Gnaw, gnaw, gnaw.* It was hard work, and her jaws got tired, but she didn't stop.

Leo watched in amazement. For hours, the little mouse chewed and chewed, one strand at a time.

Finally, with a loud *SNAP*, the biggest rope broke. The net loosened enough for Leo to push his way out. He was free!

He stretched his great golden body and looked down at the tiny mouse, who was panting and tired.

"Thank you, Milly," the lion said, his voice soft and warm now. "I am sorry I laughed at you. You saved my life. You are a very brave and clever friend."

"You are welcome, Leo," said Milly with a smile. "I told you, even small creatures can help."

From that day on, the mighty lion and the tiny mouse were the best of friends. No one laughed at the idea anymore, because they had all seen it with their own eyes: the smallest help can be the biggest help of all.

**Moral of the story: No one is too small or too mighty to help another. Kindness is never wasted.**
