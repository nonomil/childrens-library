# The Ugly Duckling

Once upon a time, on a big farm near a green meadow, a mother duck was sitting on her eggs. They were warm and safe under her. "Soon," she quacked softly, "my little ones will hatch."

One sunny morning, the eggs began to crack. One by one, fluffy yellow ducklings popped out. They looked around with bright, curious eyes. The mother duck was very happy.

But one egg was different. It was big and grey. Crak! Finally, a very large, fluffy grey bird tumbled out. He had a long neck and big feet. He did not look like the other little yellow ducklings at all.

"Oh my," said the mother duck. "You are... different."

The other animals on the farm looked too. The cows mooed, "He is not a duck." The chickens clucked, "He is too big and grey!" The other ducklings whispered, "He is ugly. He cannot be one of us."

The little grey bird felt sad. He tried to play with the other ducklings, but they swam away from him. "Go away, ugly duckling!" they said.

So the ugly duckling flew over the farm fence, all by himself. His heart was heavy.

He walked through the tall grass and found a pond with wild ducks. "Can I play with you?" he asked.

The wild ducks looked at him. "You are very strange," they said. "But if you can swim fast, you may stay."

The ugly duckling swam as fast as he could with his big feet. But suddenly—BANG! BANG! Hunters were coming! The wild ducks flew away. The poor ugly duckling hid in the tall reeds. He was very scared and very lonely.

He wandered for many days. He slept in a barn, but a kind woman and her cat and hen chased him away. "You cannot lay eggs like the hen, and you cannot purr like the cat," said the woman. "You are useless."

The ugly duckling felt his heart would break. "I am just ugly," he whispered to himself. "Nobody wants me."

Winter came. The pond froze. The ugly duckling was cold and hungry. He almost froze in the ice, but a farmer saved him and took him home to his children. The children wanted to play with him, but they were too rough. They chased him, and he fell out of a box. He flew out the door as fast as he could, back into the cold snow.

He thought, "Maybe I will never have a friend."

But then, spring arrived. The ice melted. Flowers bloomed. The ugly duckling felt a warmth in his heart. He saw three beautiful swans swimming in a lake. They were so graceful and kind.

"I want to be like them," he thought. "Even if they chase me away, it is better to be chased by such beautiful creatures."

So he swam towards them. He bowed his head, ready to be told to go away.

But the swans did not chase him. They swam right up to him.

"Hello, friend!" they said kindly.

The ugly duckling was confused. Then he looked down at the water. He saw his reflection. But it was not a grey, ugly bird he saw. He saw a magnificent white swan with a long, graceful neck!

He was not an ugly duckling at all. He had always been a swan, just like the others. He just needed time to grow.

The other swans welcomed him with gentle pecks. From that day on, he swam with his new friends. He was beautiful, and more importantly, he was happy and loved.

***

**The Moral:** You are special and wonderful just as you are. Sometimes, you have to grow into your true self. Be patient and kind to yourself and others, because true beauty comes from within.
