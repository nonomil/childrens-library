# The Three Little Pigs

Once upon a time, in a bright green meadow, three little pigs left their mother’s cozy home to build houses of their own.

“I will build my house today!” said the first little pig. He found some soft, yellow straw. *Whoosh, whoosh!* He built a little house in just one hour. “Now I can play all day!” he laughed, dancing around his straw house.

“I will build my house this week,” said the second little pig. He gathered long, thin sticks. *Tap, tap, tap!* He built a house with a wobbly door. It took him three days. “Good enough!” he said, and went to play with his brother.

But the third little pig was different. “I will build a house that lasts,” he said wisely. He found red, hard bricks and gray, sticky mortar. *Clank, scrape, clank.* Every day he worked from sunrise to sunset. It took him a whole month! But finally, he had a strong, solid brick house with a sturdy chimney.

One day, a big, bad wolf came trotting from the dark woods. He saw the first pig’s straw house. *Knock, knock!* “Little pig, little pig, let me come in!” he howled.

“Not by the hair on my chinny-chin-chin!” squeaked the little pig.

The wolf took a deep breath. **“Then I’ll huff, and I’ll puff, and I’ll blow your house in!”** And he did! The straw house flew away like dandelion seeds. The first little pig ran as fast as his legs could carry him, right to his brother’s stick house.

Soon, the wolf was there too. *Knock, knock!* “Little pigs, little pigs, let me come in!”

“Not by the hair on our chinny-chin-chins!” they cried together.

The wolf laughed. **“Then I’ll huff, and I’ll puff, and I’ll blow your house in!”** He huffed and puffed until the sticks fell down like pick-up sticks. The two pigs squealed and ran all the way to their brother’s brick house.

The wolf followed them. *Knock, knock!* “Little pigs, little pigs, let me come in!” his voice was very deep now.

“NOT BY THE HAIR ON OUR CHINNY-CHIN-CHINS!” they shouted, huddled together.

The wolf was angry. **“Then I’ll huff, and I’ll PUFF, and I’ll BLOW YOUR HOUSE IN!”** He blew with all his might. He blew so hard his cheeks turned red. But the brick house didn’t move. Not even a little. He was tired and puzzled.

Then he saw the chimney. “Aha!” he thought. He climbed onto the roof and started to slide down.

But the clever third little pig had been ready. He put a big pot of water on the fire. *Gurgle, gurgle, bubble!* When the wolf slid down, he landed right in the hot water!

“Yowch!” yelled the wolf. He jumped up and ran away as fast as he could, all the way back to the dark woods, and never bothered the pigs again.

The three little pigs danced with joy in their safe, strong house. They had learned that taking time to do things right was the best way to be safe and happy.

**Moral:** Hard work and careful planning protect us and help us build a safe, happy home.
