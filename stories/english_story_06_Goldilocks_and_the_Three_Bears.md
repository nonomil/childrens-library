# Goldilocks and the Three Bears

Once upon a time, in a cheerful green forest, lived a family of three bears. There was a big Papa Bear, a medium-sized Mama Bear, and a tiny Baby Bear. They had a cozy little house with a roof made of moss and a door painted sunny yellow.

One morning, Mama Bear made a delicious pot of warm porridge for breakfast. “It’s too hot to eat right now,” she said. “Let’s go for a little walk while it cools down.”

So the three bears went out into the sunny forest, leaving their porridge on the table.

Now, not far from their house lived a little girl named Goldilocks. She had golden curls and a very curious mind. While wandering through the woods, she spotted the cute little house. “What a lovely cottage!” she thought. She looked around, saw no one, and peeked inside.

The door was open. Goldilocks stepped in and sniffed the air. “Mmm, something smells wonderful!” she said.

She saw the three bowls of porridge on the table. There was a very large bowl, a medium-sized bowl, and a tiny little bowl. Being hungry, she decided to try some.

She took a spoonful from Papa Bear’s big bowl. “Yuck! This porridge is too hot!” she said, blowing on her tongue.

Then she tried Mama Bear’s bowl. “Bleh! This porridge is too cold,” she decided.

Finally, she tasted the porridge from Baby Bear’s little bowl. “Ah, this is just right!” She ate it all up.

Feeling full, Goldilocks noticed three chairs by the fireplace. She sat in the big chair. “This chair is too hard!” she said, wiggling uncomfortably. She sat in the medium chair. “This chair is too soft,” she sighed, sinking into the cushions.

Then she sat in Baby Bear’s tiny chair. “This chair is just right!” But as she sat, *CRACK!* The chair broke into pieces under her. “Oh dear,” she whispered.

A little sleepy now, Goldilocks went upstairs. She saw three beds. She lay down on the big bed. “This bed is too long!” She tried the medium bed. “This bed is too wide!” Then she snuggled into Baby Bear’s little bed. “This bed is just right.” She fell fast asleep.

Meanwhile, the three bears returned home. “Someone’s been eating my porridge!” boomed Papa Bear in his deep voice.

“Someone’s been eating my porridge too!” said Mama Bear.

“Someone’s eaten ALL my porridge!” cried Baby Bear in a tiny voice.

They looked at their chairs. “Someone’s been sitting in my chair!” grumbled Papa Bear.

“Someone’s been sitting in my chair!” said Mama Bear.

“Someone’s sat in my chair and BROKEN it!” wailed Baby Bear.

Quietly, the bears tiptoed upstairs. “Someone’s been sleeping in my bed!” said Papa Bear.

“Someone’s been sleeping in my bed!” said Mama Bear.

“Someone’s sleeping in MY bed right now!” gasped Baby Bear.

Goldilocks woke up with a start. She saw three pairs of wide eyes staring at her. “Yikes!” she yelped, jumping out of the tiny bed. “I’m so sorry! I didn’t mean to cause trouble. I was just very curious.”

The bears saw she was scared and not mean. Papa Bear’s voice was gentle. “Curiosity is fine,” he said, “but we don’t take things that aren’t ours without asking.”

“We always clean up our mess,” added Mama Bear kindly.

Baby Bear just looked sadly at his broken chair.

Goldilocks felt very sorry. “I will help fix your chair,” she promised, “and I’ll make you new porridge.” And she did.

**The moral of the story is:** It’s important to respect other people’s homes and belongings. Always ask before taking something that isn’t yours, and if you make a mistake, be brave enough to say you’re sorry and help fix it.
