# The Ant and the Grasshopper

In Sunny Meadow, where the grass was soft and the sun was warm, lived two little friends. Andy Ant was very busy. He worked all day, carrying crumbs of cookie and seeds of dandelion back to his little hill. 

“Hurry, hurry!” he would say to himself. “Winter is coming. I must be ready!”

Gracie Grasshopper loved to dance. She would leap from blade to blade of grass, singing, “Tra-la-la! What a beautiful day!”

“Hello, Andy!” Gracie sang one sunny afternoon. “Stop working and dance with me! The sun is shining!”

Andy wiped his forehead with his tiny leg. “I can’t, Gracie. I need to gather food for the cold winter.”

Gracie laughed. “Winter is far away! There is plenty of time. Play with me today!”

Andy shook his head. “No, thank you. Work first, then play.”

Every day was the same. Andy worked and worked. He gathered grains of wheat and sweet apple seeds. His storage room under the hill got fuller and fuller. 

Gracie played and played. She made flower-chain necklaces and sang with the crickets. She didn’t think about winter at all.

The leaves turned red and yellow. The air grew cool. Gracie still danced. “Winter is still far away!” she said.

Then one morning, frost glittered on the grass. The trees were almost bare. Gracie looked for something to eat. But all the seeds and berries were gone. Her tummy rumbled. “Oh dear,” she said. “I am very hungry.”

She saw Andy’s little hill. A warm, yummy smell came from inside. Gracie knocked on Andy’s door with a shaky leaf. 

*Knock, knock.*

The door opened. Andy Ant was wearing a cozy sweater. 

“Andy,” Gracie whispered. “I have no food. Winter came too fast. Could you… could you please share some?”

Andy looked at Gracie’s thin legs and sad eyes. He felt a little sorry for her. 

“Come in, Gracie,” he said kindly.

Inside, Andy’s home was warm. His storage room was full of yummy food. He made Gracie a tiny soup from seeds and a piece of dried apple.

“Thank you, Andy,” Gracie said quietly. “You were so wise to get ready.”

Andy smiled. “I worked hard because I knew winter would come. Next year, maybe we can prepare together? We could gather food in the morning, and then dance in the afternoon.”

Gracie’s eyes lit up. “Yes! That would be perfect. I learned my lesson.”

That winter, Gracie helped Andy by telling him funny stories while he worked. When spring came, they worked together every morning. And every afternoon? They danced together in the warm sun, and Gracie’s songs were the happiest in all of Sunny Meadow.

**The Moral:** It is wise to prepare for tomorrow while enjoying today. Work and play can go together, but a little planning keeps us safe and happy.
