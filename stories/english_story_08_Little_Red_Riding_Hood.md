# Little Red Riding Hood

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl who always wore a bright red hooded cape. Everyone called her Little Red Riding Hood. She lived in a cozy cottage with her mother.

One sunny morning, her mother said, “Little Red, your grandmother is feeling a bit lonely. Could you take her this basket of fresh bread and jam? Walk straight to her house, and don’t dawdle in the forest.”

Little Red nodded. “I promise, Mama!”

The forest was full of chirping birds and tall, friendly trees. As she skipped along the path, she saw a patch of beautiful wildflowers. “Grandma would love these!” she thought. She stopped to pick a small bouquet, forgetting all about going straight to Grandma’s house.

While she was picking flowers, a sly wolf stepped onto the path. “Good morning, little girl,” he said with a toothy grin. “Where are you going all alone?”

Little Red, who was taught to be polite, said, “I’m going to my grandmother’s house. She lives in the little cottage with the red roof, just beyond the big oak tree.”

The wolf’s eyes twinkled with a naughty idea. He hurried away to the cottage and knocked on the door. “Grandma, it’s me, Little Red!” he called in a high, squeaky voice.

Grandma, who was not feeling well, said, “Come in, dear. The door is unlocked.”

When the wolf tiptoed inside, Grandma got a big fright! The wolf quickly locked her in a cupboard and then put on her nightgown and cap. He hopped into her bed and pulled the quilt up to his nose.

Soon, Little Red arrived at the cottage. She noticed the door was open. “Hello, Grandma?” she said as she walked in.

“My, what big eyes you have!” said Little Red.

“All the better to see you with, my dear,” the wolf replied in a shaky voice.

“My, what big ears you have!”

“All the better to hear you with, my dear.”

“My… what big teeth you have!” Little Red said, suddenly feeling a little nervous.

“All the better to EAT you with!” roared the wolf, jumping out of bed.

Just then, a kind huntsman was walking by. He heard the commotion and rushed inside. With a brave shout, he surprised the wolf so much that the wolf ran away, all the way back to the deep, dark forest. He never returned to the cottage again.

The huntsman gently opened the cupboard and freed Grandma. She was a little shaken but perfectly safe. Little Red gave her grandmother a huge hug and a basket of bread and jam.

“I’m so sorry I didn’t walk straight here,” Little Red said. “I learned a very important lesson today.”

From that day on, Little Red always listened to her mother and never stopped to talk to strangers in the forest.

***

**Moral:** Always listen to your parents and be careful of strangers. It’s important to stay on the path and be aware of who you are talking to.
