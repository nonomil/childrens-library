# The Tortoise and the Hare

### The Tortoise and the Hare

Once upon a time, in a sunny green meadow, lived a very fast hare named Harry. He had long, twitchy ears and could run like the wind. "I'm the fastest creature in the whole forest!" Harry would boast, zipping past the trees.

At the edge of the meadow lived a tortoise named Tara. She had a big, strong shell and moved very, very slowly. *Thump… thump… thump* went her feet on the ground.

One morning, Harry saw Tara slowly munching on a juicy leaf. "Hello, slowpoke!" he laughed. "You are the slowest thing I have ever seen!"

Tara finished her leaf calmly. "Fast or slow, we all have our own way of moving, Harry."

Harry twitched his nose. "I bet I could run to the Big Oak Tree and back before you even cross the flower patch! Want to have a race?"

A small crowd gathered to watch. The squirrel, the birds, and even the busy ants stopped to cheer. "Ready… set… GO!" shouted the old owl.

*WHOOSH!* Harry disappeared in a flash of brown fur. Tara took a deep breath and started her steady *thump… thump… thump*.

Harry was so far ahead, he could see the Big Oak Tree already. "Ha! This is too easy!" he said. He looked back. Tara was just a tiny speck. "I have plenty of time for a nap."

So, under the warm sun, Harry curled up under a bush and fell fast asleep.

Meanwhile, Tara kept going. Her legs were tired, and her shell felt heavy, but she didn't stop. *Thump… thump… thump.* She passed the sleeping Harry without making a sound.

The sun began to set, painting the sky orange and pink. The little ant shouted, "Go, Tara, go!"

Harry suddenly woke up. "Oh no!" he cried. He ran faster than ever, his legs a blur.

But when he arrived at the finish line under the Big Oak Tree, who was there? Tara, smiling and holding a little victory flower.

The whole meadow cheered for the tortoise! Harry’s ears drooped with shame. "I'm sorry, Tara," he said quietly. "I was too proud. I thought being fast was everything."

Tara nodded kindly. "It’s not about how fast you go, Harry. It’s about never giving up and keeping on going, step by step."

**The Moral:** Slow and steady wins the race. It's not about being the fastest, but about being determined and finishing what you start.
